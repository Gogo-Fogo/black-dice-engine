# Campaign Design

Black Dice Engine needs a campaign design layer before it can GM well.

Without this layer, the AI can improvise fantasy scenes, but it will not understand what kind of game the table is building, where the story is headed, or what should remain hidden.

## Campaign Setup Wizard

The GM dashboard should eventually guide setup through:

1. Choose campaign type.
2. Choose tone and content rating.
3. Choose length, player count, and level range.
4. Define early/mid/endgame.
5. Define main factions, villains, and mysteries.
6. Define player backstory hooks.
7. Generate starting location and opening scene.
8. Generate shared art style.
9. Generate first session plan.

## Core Modules

- `Campaign Bible`: world facts, tone, pillars, house rules, major truths.
- `Act Planner`: early/mid/endgame structure, expected escalation, level ranges.
- `Quest Graph`: active/locked/resolved quests and clues.
- `Pacing Manager`: tracks combat, roleplay, horror, mystery, downtime, rewards, and reveals.
- `Reveal Manager`: protects GM-only secrets and staged reveals.
- `Progression Tracker`: milestones, factions discovered, bosses defeated, player hooks resolved.
- `Session Generator`: prep assistant for likely scenes, NPCs, encounters, clues, and image prompts.
- `Memory Canonizer`: turns scene/session outcomes into compact campaign memory.

## Presets

Game type presets should tune the system:

- dark fantasy campaign
- heroic fantasy campaign
- horror one-shot
- political intrigue
- dungeon crawl
- sandbox hexcrawl
- monster hunter
- villain campaign
- romance/drama heavy
- survival wilderness

Each preset can affect pacing, encounter frequency, tone, image style, rules strictness, NPC behavior, reward structure, danger level, and mature-content defaults.

## Content Library

The AI should not have to invent the whole campaign from scratch.

The tool should eventually support:

- original campaign bibles
- open/SRD-safe rules and references
- open-license adventures and bestiaries
- private uploaded PDFs
- manual notes
- player backstories
- AI-generated adaptation glue

Every import needs source, license, visibility, and attribution metadata. Public demos must use only original or safely licensed material.
