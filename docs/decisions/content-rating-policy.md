# Content Rating Policy

Black Dice Engine should support mature dark fantasy campaigns through explicit campaign settings, not hardcoded behavior.

## Principle

The system should be adult-capable, locally customizable, and table-consensual.

Mature content should be controlled by campaign and scene settings. It should not be constantly injected just because it is allowed.

## Content Rating Controls

Campaigns should define intensity levels for:

- violence
- horror
- gore
- profanity
- romance
- sexual content
- hopelessness
- comedy relief

Example scale: `0` means absent, `5` means very high intensity.

## Mature Campaign Defaults

A mature dark fantasy campaign may allow:

- brutal consequences
- horror
- moral ambiguity
- adult politics
- profanity
- romance and seduction when narratively appropriate
- consensual adult sexual themes with configurable explicitness

But it should still avoid forced, constant, or tonally random sexualization.

## Hard Boundaries

Committed prompts, schemas, and validators should enforce hard boundaries such as:

- no sexual content involving minors
- no eroticized coercion
- no sexual violence as entertainment
- no inability-to-consent sexual content
- no real-world extremist propaganda
- respect table pause/rewind/fade-to-black settings

## Dashboard Setting

The GM dashboard should eventually support a simple mode such as:

```text
Sexual Content: Off | Suggestive | Mature | Explicit With Confirmation
```

The project default should be mature-capable but tasteful: adult themes can exist, while explicit content requires deliberate configuration and confirmation.

## Public Repo Rule

Do not commit private/adult table content, private generated assets, explicit campaign logs, or player secrets. Public demo content should remain original, demo-safe, and appropriate for a public repository.
