# Image Prompt Templates

These templates are placeholders for local ComfyUI workflows. Generated images should be stored outside this repository.

## Scene Establishing Shot

```text
Dark cinematic tabletop RPG scene, {location}, {time_of_day}, {weather}, strong composition, practical details, grounded fantasy atmosphere, no text, no watermark
```

## NPC Portrait

```text
Portrait of {npc_name}, {role}, {distinctive_features}, moody practical lighting, grounded dark fantasy, expressive face, no text, no watermark
```

## Prop Or Clue

```text
Detailed close-up of {object}, worn material texture, table prop style, dramatic side lighting, no text, no watermark
```

## Safety Notes

- Avoid real player likenesses unless explicitly approved.
- Avoid copyrighted characters, logos, or proprietary setting references.
- Keep prompts aligned with the active content rating.
